# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

EasyPanel welcomes contributors regardless of experience or background. Be respectful,
specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and repeated behavior when deciding a response.
